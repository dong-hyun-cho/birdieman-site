Organizer guide · about 10 minutes

Set up the Turkey Shoot

Follow the screens one by one. The yellow words are exactly what you tap.

Before you start

Open birdieman.co.kr/app on your phone or computer, or the BirdieMan app on iPhone. Sign in with Apple, Google or email (organizers only; players never sign in). Have the team list ready, in Excel or typed out.

Create the tournament

1Open the Events tab

Tap the trophy icon at the bottom, then Create an event.

2Pick River Bend

Tap Find a course, type River, and tap River Bend GC (Camp Humphreys).

Pars and yardages from the official scorecard are already loaded.

3Date and names

Tap Other day and pick 22 in November. Then tap Rename and enter:

Club name: River Bend Golf Course
Tournament name: 2026 Turkey Shoot

Tap Next · Players.

4Paste the teams

Copy your team sheet from Excel, one row per team, and paste it into the box. Then tap Add.

Team 1Mike ReyesChris WalkerJason KimTony Brooks
Team 2Dave MillerRyan ScottKevin ParkMatt Hughes
The “Team 1” column is optional; it's ignored. You can also just type names separated by commas.

5Check the teams

Each row became one group. To move someone, tap their name and then the group it should go to. Tap Next · Format.

6Choose Scramble

Tap Scramble (group = team, one team score per hole), check the summary, and tap Create & get link.

7Turn on Shotgun start

On the Groups tab, switch on Shotgun start. Group 1 starts on #1, Group 2 on #2, and so on. Use − / ＋ to change a team's starting hole.

Each team's scorecard then starts on its own hole and finishes on the hole before it, for example #7 → #18 → #1 → #6.

8Send the link to players

Tap Send link to players and share it by text, email or group chat, together with the player instructions.

Only one person per team needs to keep score. Players don't need the app or an account.

On tournament day

9Put the board on the clubhouse TV

Tap Show big on Clubhouse TV, then Send link and open that address on the TV or a laptop connected to it. It updates by itself and shows big alerts for birdies and eagles.

10Watch the leaderboard

Hole = holes played (F = finished). ✎ = a score was changed. ⏱ = several holes were entered at once. Both are visible to everyone.

If something looks off, compare it with that team's paper card.

11Close the tournament

When the last team is in, scroll down to Organizer. Enter Closest to Pin and Longest Drive winners, choose how many Raffle winners to draw, and tap Close tournament.

The raffle is drawn at random and skips the top-3 teams and the CTP / Longest Drive winners.

12Announce the awards

The Awards tab shows team standings, CTP, Longest Drive and raffle winners. Put it on the TV during dinner, or share it as an image.
